A friendly daily call, just to be sure.

The Safety Check-In Program gives residents of Kings County who live alone a reassuring phone call every single day — and makes sure someone notices right away if a call goes unanswered.

Sign Up / Express Interest Call (902) 670-0725

What the program does

A no-cost public safety program for Kings County residents aged 55 and over who live alone and have no daily in-home care.

Daily phone call

A volunteer calls every participant each morning between 9:00 am and noon, seven days a week.

15 rings, then action

If there's no answer after 15 rings, the volunteer calls your emergency contacts right away.

Peace of mind

Added security and independence for participants — and reassurance for the family who worries about them.

How a check-in day works

1

You share two emergency contacts

When you enroll, you give us the names and information of at least two people we can contact if needed.

2

A volunteer calls, 9 am–noon

Every day of the week, a volunteer calls you to say hello and confirm everything is okay.

3

No answer after 15 rings?

The volunteer calls your first emergency contact to check in on you.

4

Still can't reach anyone?

An appropriate emergency service is contacted on your behalf.

Please note: this is not an immediate life-safety service. It's designed for regular, reassuring daily checkups — not emergency response.

Enrollment, step by step

1

Call the Seniors' Safety Director at (902) 670-0725 to request an application.

2

Return your completed application and waiver to the Kings Seniors' Safety staff.

3

Confirm your start date — staff will call to confirm and go over program specifics.

4

You're added to the volunteer calling list.

5

Your daily calls begin!

About Kings Seniors' Safety

Since 2006, Kings Seniors' Safety has been a guiding hand for seniors throughout Nova Scotia's Kings County. Our goals are to help people feel connected to their community, provide useful safety and security information, and connect service providers to seniors to aid with local projects and events.
